Decisions

Six calls to approve first

Condensed milk is the sugar for Kopi and Teh. Kopi / Teh = condensed milk only; siew dai = less condensed, ga dai = more. The sugar can is for O and C drinks. That's how a real uncle makes it, and it means one less pour. "Kopi kosong" isn't on the menu (customers say "Kopi C kosong"). Recommended
The pour bar docks under the clock, full width. While you pour, a big labelled bar shows at the top of the screen, so your finger is never on it. The small meter beside the cup goes away. Recommended
Liquid becomes HD art, tinted and cropped by code. It replaces the flat drawn rectangles. I'll draw an HD-quality stand-in myself so we're not blocked, and swap in your art when it arrives. Recommended
Starter menu + unlocks. Day 1 serves 6 bases × kosong / siew dai / normal × hot / peng, plus Milo drinks. Everything else unlocks from the night shop or by fame. Recommended
Night shop after each day's summary, from Day 3. Items are gated by fame tiers (0 / 25 / 50 / 75), so the month has a steady drip of new toys. Recommended
Malt drink name. "Horlicks" is what people actually order, but it's a brand, like the Toto issue before. Call it "Malt" in game, keeping Milo as it is already. Recommended: "Malt"

Time of day

A real wall clock, plus the day as a bar

The hour hand sweeps from 7am to 7pm across the 90-second day; the minute hand spins so time visibly moves. Under it, the day as a bar with labelled periods. The marker crawls along it, and the clock rings a bell at last orders.
