Drink Pour Prototype

The new filling animation on the real glass from the game, with the pour bar as it will dock above the customers. The drink's surface and bottom follow the mug's oval perspective, and the surface tilts and sloshes as it settles. Pick a drink; it plays on a loop. Nothing here is in the game yet.

Pour bar

What's new: the liquid sits inside the glass with 2-tone shading, a wobbling surface and the glass's shine over it. Streams, milk clouds, sugar, ice splashes and steam replace the flat coloured box.

Ice: the code-drawn pixel cubes from the plan, dropped with a splash and bobbing at the surface.

Pour bar: zones touch with no gaps; the yellow outline is the order's target. It trembles in GA DAI and shakes harder when TOO SWEET (try the last button).

Colours: Kopi O near-black, Kopi golden caramel, Kopi C lighter and creamier, Teh O amber-red, Teh orange caramel, Teh C peachy beige. The game uses the same palette.
