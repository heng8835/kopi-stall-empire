## The gaps, ranked

### P0: breaks trust

1. **The game froze on the morning after any day with a goal. Fixed in `b254dfa` and live.**
   - Hit by every player who crossed Day 2 → 3 in one sitting without reloading; Mary stopped before it.
   - Cause: the scene object is reused each day, and `goalChip` wasn't reset, so the morning wrote to yesterday's destroyed text.
   - This was a round-5 regression. Pre-push checks jumped to a day by URL and never crossed from one day to the next.
   - Checked after the fix: three day changes in a row without a crash, and Ken's whole run. `tests/sceneReset.test.js` guards the reset.
   - *Still to do:* always cross a real day in the pre-push check.
2. **The Milo Dinosaur recipe card still comes after the kids are served (Wei Jie, Priya, Lena, Sam, Ken, Alex).**
   - The round-5 fix covered teachers who walk in, but not lessons taught after a story (`afterStory`, Day 2), so the kids order, get served, EP2 plays, and then the card appears.
   - *Fix:* hold the story guest's order until the card and story are done, or show the card first.
3. **Gestures leak into stories and cards (Ken, Wei Jie, Mary, Lena, Alex).**
   - An episode starting mid-drag: the drag's taps skip lines to the choice with no context, and one tap picked a choice (Mary).
   - A recipe card swallowed a dial tap and a serve (Ken, Daniel).
   - The episode Skip sits over Recipes (Alex).
   - *Fix:* never start a story or card while a cup is in hand; ignore input for about 500 ms after one opens; don't advance on a drag release; move Skip.
4. **A held teacher can block the counter (Daniel, Sam, Ken).**
   - The card waits for 1.8 s of untouched screen, which a busy player rarely gives. Daniel waited about 35 s, and Sam's Day 4 hold ran from 10:30am to 6pm game time.
   - A round-5 side effect. *Fix:* show the card when the teacher arrives, with only an in-progress drag delaying it.
5. **The first drag is where a non-gamer quits (Raj).**
   - The Day-1 demo stops at "Done! Drag it to Aunty Lim". Raj tapped, long-pressed and waited over a minute with no response.
   - *Fix:* the hand carries the cup all the way; tapping a finished cup gives a hint; avoid the word "drag"; consider tap-cup-then-tap-person.
6. **A purchase is lost silently (Lena, Raj, Sam, Daniel).** Buy changes to "Confirm?", and leaving after one tap buys nothing, with no warning. *Fix:* one-tap buy with an undo, or warn on Open stall.

### P1: makes it feel thin or unfair

**Design**

7. **The Empire goal is reached on Day 7 of 30 (Sam, Ken).**
   - Fame rises 7–11 a day. "名气 70: a second stall" is met with 23 days left, so the round-5 teaser now advertises a broken target.
   - Fame also stops buying customers at 15, so decor is pointless.
   - *Fix:* retune the fame curve or the gate (Ken suggests reaching it around Day 25–28), add a tracker, and give fame a job after 50.
8. **Difficulty goes flat after Day 4; goals complete themselves (Sam, Wei Jie, Ken).**
   - Nobody walked out in days of play, though players with fixed timings overstate this. Queued customers don't drain, and 7pm removes time pressure.
   - "Serve 4 hot drinks" was done by 11am. "Nobody walks out" is "free money".
   - Mary, however, felt flustered from 11am, and Raj's pour window is tight. The challenge needs to be *optional*, not just harder.
   - *Fix:* stretch goals and a personal best, not tighter base timers.
9. **Pay runs against effort; tips overtake sales (Ken, Sam, Raj).**
   - One-step Kopi O kosong earned up to $4.40; five-step Kopi peng $2.65–3.35. Tips beat sales by Day 3–6.
   - The $12 Tip Jar is the obvious buy. Machines don't show drink prices, so nothing can be worked out before buying.
   - Raj adds that nobody tips at a real kopitiam.
10. **No mastery layer (Sam, Ken).** Each pour is a fixed hold, solved in about 10 cups. Long-Spout breaks timing without saying so, and Milk Pump has no way back. *Ideas:* a Perfect+ sweet spot, a daily best, toggles on tools.
11. **Friendship is the biggest unused hook (Ken).** The hearts are never explained (Raj, Ken). *Idea:* a regulars screen with levels, favourites and unlocks.

**Screen and teaching**

12. **Round-5 changes that need a second look:**
    - **Pour meter at the top** now clips the order line in bubbles (Ken, Wei Jie, Nurul, Alex) and is far from the cup and the thumb (Mary, Raj, Daniel). Faces are clear, but the problem has moved.
    - **Coach text shrinks to tiny** when the goal chip narrows it, and long lines are the ones players need most (Mary, Wei Jie, Alex, Daniel).
    - **Coach says pot first** while the demo and recipe book say milk first (Lena, Raj, Daniel, Alex, Wei Jie, Priya). I changed the demo and not the coach or the Day-1 card.
    - **Goal chip "0 left"** on "Nobody walks out" is misread by nearly everyone. Try "0 walked out".
13. **Twists don't look like what they say (Wei Jie, Lena, Nurul, Alex).** "Rain day" shows a sunny street, Makcik says "Panas lah today", and iced orders still come. Only the office rush changed how anyone played (Sam).
14. **EP2's note is wrong (Mary, Lena, Sam, Ken).** "The kids pay $9.80 and owe you 20¢" appears after they've already paid about $15. A round-5 addition: the kids are served before the episode plays.
15. **Too many cards and taps around a day (Priya, Wei Jie, Daniel, Ken, Lena).** Up to 5 cards in a morning, plus a results card of 12–18 lines. *Fix:* one morning card, about 5 results lines.
16. **Pour window is tight when learning (Mary, Raj, Priya, Daniel).** About 1.1 s falls short and 1.4–1.5 s spills, and the demo never lets you feel the hold. *Fix:* a forgiving first attempt.
17. **Coach and cup can disagree (Mary, Raj).** The coach names one customer and the new cup goes to another. Dropping a cup on the wrong person delivers it to the labelled one, which is kind but teaches nothing, and "for Ah Gong" means little if anyone can get it (Wei Jie, Lena, Raj).
18. **Teaching order (Ken, Alex, Wei Jie).** Milo Dinosaur on Day 2 but plain Milo on Day 6; peng taught after a peng drink; Kopi C kosong before Kopi C, which caused an Oops. Lena never poured a siew dai in three days.

**Language and culture**

19. **Character voices (Nurul).**
    - The Indian-looking "Uncle" says "Boss, Teh tarik one. 慢慢来." This is the top representation issue; it looks like a shared generic line, and he has no name.
    - Makcik should say "kopi ais", not "kopi peng", and "Panas lah today" doesn't fit a hot teh tarik order. Makcik and Uncle have no names while Chinese characters do.
    - "Hawker next door" is a Chinese man with a wok, yet EP4 makes Makcik the neighbour. No pakcik appears in six days though the shop keeps promising them, and no Indian customer appeared in two days for Raj.
    - The glossary defines pakcik by a stereotype.
20. **Chinese-only lines carry jokes foreigners miss (Lena, Raj).** 有进步空间, 我们看看, 你很乖. Black text looks untappable, and some glossary entries just repeat the word. "嗯. 好. (Mm. Good.)" already shows the format to use.
21. **Lena's asks:** pronunciation and a "My words" list in the recipe book; fade the coach's full recipe once a drink is known, so the customer's words matter.
22. **Aunty Lim:** a 10/10 on Day 1 kills the gag, right after she rates Uncle Tan 9/10 (Nurul). She praises a teh served Too Sweet (Ken), and an unexplained 6/10 and 8/10 can appear beside "14 of 15" and "15 of 15" perfect (Alex, Ken).
23. **Details regulars notice (Mary, Raj, Nurul):** hot kopi in a glass mug, not thick china; the chope tissue icon is a toilet roll (noted by 6 players); siew dai should be labelled Hokkien.

## Recommended next steps (in order)

1. **P0 #2–#6:** the Milo card, input leaks, the held teacher, the first drag, the lost purchase.
2. **Tidy what round 5 broke (#12–#14):** the meter over orders, shrinking coach text, coach order, the chip wording, a rain day that looks rainy, EP2's note.
3. **A plan of its own for the economy (#7–#11):** fame curve, pay against effort, optional mastery, friendship. This is what should move fun.
4. **A language pass (#19–#23),** ideally with a real Singaporean reader. Nurul's notes are from a simulated player and should be checked with people.
5. **Fewer cards and a shorter results screen (#15).**
6. **Put the build in front of a few real people.** Ten simulated players agree on a lot, but none has a real thumb.

## Method notes

- Ten agents, one browser tab each, their own save namespace, a fresh start from the title screen. Nine ran in parallel for up to about 25 minutes; Ken ran afterwards.
- **Browser cap:** the pane allows about 9 tabs, and finished testers leave theirs open. Ken's first attempt couldn't get a tab in about 20 minutes and produced no review, which turned out useful: Ken's is the one run without the freeze.
- **Cost:** about 2.1M agent tokens for the first nine, plus about 73k for Ken's blocked attempt and Ken's full run. Roughly double round 5.
